# Information for participants — template (chatbot on our own computer)

Give this notice to the participants before they use the chatbot (on paper, on a slide, or on your
group's page). Replace each `TO BE COMPLETED: …` with your text, and have it checked by your
organisation's data protection officer. You can translate it into the language of your group.
For participants under 18, also check what your organisation asks of their parents or guardians.

---

## Using the chatbot of our group

**What it is.** A chatbot set up by TO BE COMPLETED: who (for example: "your youth workers") for
TO BE COMPLETED: the activity or topic. It is an artificial intelligence (AI), a computer
program, not a person. It answers from the documents we gave it. It can make mistakes: check
important answers in the documents, or ask us.

**When you can use it.** TO BE COMPLETED: one of these (for example: "only during our sessions" or
"any time you are on our Wi-Fi").

**Your account.** You create it yourself when you open the address: click **Sign up** under the
sign-in form. As your name, use a first name or a nickname, never your full name. As e-mail, use an
address that does not identify you (for example `nickname7@group.local`; each address can be used
only once). Choose a **new password**, not one you use elsewhere: you need it again next time. Do
not write personal information about yourself or other people in your questions.

**What is stored, and where.**
- The chatbot runs on a computer of TO BE COMPLETED: the organisation, in TO BE COMPLETED: where
  (for example: "our youth centre"). You reach it through our Wi-Fi. The connection is not
  encrypted: someone on the same Wi-Fi could see what you type. This is one more reason to choose a
  new password, and to keep personal information out of your questions.
- Your account, your conversations and your questions stay on this computer. The AI model that
  answers runs on this computer too: your questions are not sent to an AI company.
- When you click a thumb (up or down) under an answer, your rating, your comment if you write one,
  and a copy of the conversation are kept for TO BE COMPLETED: who (for example: "your youth
  workers"), to improve the chatbot. They can also see how many questions each account asked.

**What the chatbot remembers.** TO BE COMPLETED: one of these two sentences (for example:
"Nothing: each conversation starts from zero.", only if Memory is unchecked in every chatbot,
including new ones; or: "It can remember what you tell it about yourself and use it in your next
conversations, with all the chatbots of the group. You can see and delete it in Settings →
Personalization.")

**What you can do yourself.** You can delete a conversation (its menu **…** → **Delete**), and
what the chatbot remembers about you (your name at the bottom left → **Settings** →
**Personalization**). Leave the profile photo, the bio, the gender and the birth date of your
account empty: the chatbot does not need them. On a shared tablet or computer, sign out at the end
(your name at the bottom left → **Sign Out**): closing the tab is not enough.

**Who can read your conversations.** TO BE COMPLETED: who (for example: "your youth workers, and
the person who looks after the computer"), as the administrators of the chatbot and of the
computer, can read them. The other participants cannot.

**How long.** Your account and conversations are deleted TO BE COMPLETED: when (for example: "on
30 June" or "at the end of the project").

**Questions or requests** (access, deletion): TO BE COMPLETED: a contact (youth worker or data
protection officer). You can also complain to TO BE COMPLETED: the data protection authority of
your country.
